Images and Tables in the Audiobook

The audiobook of The Second Monopoly reads the text and leaves out what a voice cannot read well: the pictures and the longest tables. Where one appears, the narrator says its item number, and for a long table gives a short summary. Every one of them is here, as printed in the book.

The Picture Section

In print, the Picture Section, The Forgotten Half in 23 Images, follows the Preface. The audiobook leaves it out.
