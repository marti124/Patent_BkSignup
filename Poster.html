The Book Poster

A book on a shelf has a few seconds and a spine. This poster gives it a picture instead — and a browser who finds their own trade on one of the nineteen spokes has a reason to pick the book up.

Printing it

The PDF is built for 12 × 18 inches at 300 dpi, which any print shop or photo counter will run for a few dollars. The same file enlarges cleanly to 18 × 27 or 24 × 36 for a window or an endcap — the type is vector, so nothing softens as it grows. Printed at letter size it still works as a counter card.

Where it earns its keep

  • Beside the book. On a shelf, an endcap, or a table — the poster explains in one image what the title cannot.
  • In a waiting room or a break room. Nineteen trades are named on it; in most rooms somebody is on the wheel.
  • At a booth or a meeting. Farm bureau, trucking association, county fair, health fair, gun show, church hall.

It is free to print and display. No permission is needed and none will be charged for.
